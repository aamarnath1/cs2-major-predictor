How the model works

  1. Rate the teamsFour rating systems learn from match results. We combine their estimates into one win chance for each matchup.
  2. Play out the MajorWe simulate the Swiss rounds and playoffs thousands of times. The Pick’em card aims for five or more correct picks.
  3. Check against past MajorsWe replay completed stages and compare how many picks the model gets right against a card based on seeds.

Research details

Model assumptions and limits
    • Ratings: we combine four team rating systems (Elo, Glicko-2, TrueSkill, Bradley-Terry) using available match history from Valve and Liquipedia.
    • Simulation: the Swiss simulation groups teams with the same record, uses the Buchholz tiebreaker and follows Valve's late-round pairing order. The live forecast uses Bo1 and Bo3 as scheduled, and the playoffs use a Bo5 final.
    • Pick'em: we try swaps and replacements to find a 10-team card with a higher simulated chance of getting 5+ correct.
    • Live: during the event, real results are locked in and only the remaining matches are simulated.
