    For the stats nerds

    Method & caveats

    How it works, what was tested, and where it can be wrong.

      How the predictions are made
      • Ratings: four classic rating systems (Elo, Glicko-2, TrueSkill, Bradley-Terry) are fitted on every pro match since CS2 launched, using Valve's ranking data and Liquipedia. Their predictions are averaged.
      • Simulation: each Swiss stage is played with the official rules: round 1 is seed 1 vs 9, later rounds pair teams on the same record using the Buchholz tiebreaker, Bo1/Bo3 as scheduled, 3 wins to advance. Stages 2 and 3 are seeded the official way, and the playoffs use a Bo5 final.
      • Pick'em: out of all possible 10-pick sets, we choose the one most likely to get 5+ correct. That's not the same as picking the 10 best teams.
      • Live: during the event, real results are locked in and only the remaining matches are simulated.
