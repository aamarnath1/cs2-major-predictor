How good is the model?

Clearly better than the official rankings at calling individual matches. For Pick'em it edges out picking by seed, but the sample is too small to be sure. Both statements are tested on real, unseen results below.

      Glossary
      Swiss stage
      16 teams. You face teams with the same record. 3 wins advances, 3 losses eliminates.
      Bo1 / Bo3 / Bo5
      Best of 1, 3 or 5 maps. More maps means the better team wins more often.
      Buchholz
      A tiebreaker: the combined record of the opponents you've played. Tougher schedule ranks higher.
      Seed
      Starting position, set by Valve's world ranking (VRS). Seed 1 meets seed 9 in round one.
      Elo / Glicko / TrueSkill / Bradley-Terry
      Four classic ways to rate teams from wins and losses. We average all four.
