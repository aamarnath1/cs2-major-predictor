Calibration

When we say 70%, is it 70%?

Predictions are grouped into 10% bins and compared with how often those teams actually won. A perfectly calibrated model sits on the dashed diagonal, and bubble size shows how many matches fall in each bin. For a Pick'em simulator, calibration matters more than raw accuracy: overconfident odds inflate 3-0 and 0-3 picks.

Assumptions

    Open research questions

      Glossary

      Log-loss
      Average of −log(probability given to what actually happened). Rewards confident correct calls, heavily punishes confident wrong ones. 0.693 = coin flipping; lower is better.
      Brier score
      Mean squared error of the probabilities. 0.25 = coin flipping; lower is better.
      Calibration error (ECE)
      Average gap between predicted and observed win rates across probability bins.
      Walk-forward
      Predict month M using only data from before month M, then move forward a month. It mimics real forecasting and prevents peeking.
      Paired bootstrap
      Resample the same test matches thousands of times and recompute the difference between two models, giving a 95% interval for that difference.
      Logit averaging
      Average the models' odds on the log-odds scale, then convert back to a probability. It's the standard way to combine probability forecasts.
      Buchholz
      Swiss tiebreaker: the summed record of a team's opponents. Used for pairing and seeding.
